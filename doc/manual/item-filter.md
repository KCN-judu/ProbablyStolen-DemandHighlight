# Item Filter

The Item Filter marks your own stock by type, stolen or contraband, whoever is at the counter. Use it to sort the shop by category.

## Opening it

Press the key bound to **Open item filter** in the game's options menu. The window is built from the game's own machine-panel widgets, so it looks like a machine window.

## The window

- **Status** is one line with Stolen and Contraband, each with its count, and Clear.
- **Types** has one toggle per item type found in the shop, with counts. These are the game's own categories, the ones customers ask for, such as Food, Medical, Tool or Luxury Item.

Types are grouped into Goods, Supplies, Gear, Machines, Grade and Restricted, the most common first within each group, and the groups are packed into balanced columns. Hover a toggle to see what it does.

The window is rebuilt only when the stock or the filter changed since it was last opened.

## Marking

- Tick anything and every matching item in the shop is marked until you clear the filter.
- Several ticks combine as "any of these".
- Type matches are drawn in cyan with the solid style. Stolen goods and contraband keep their amber and red styles.
- Containers holding matches get corner brackets.
- Locks are ignored, because the filter is for sorting your own stock.

Turning the filter on pauses the customer highlight, and clearing it brings the highlight back.
